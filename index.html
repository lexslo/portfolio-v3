Annihilated

I designed this soundscape using multiple high pass and low pass filters in Ableton Live's Wavetable instrument to create movement and suspense. One note is playing the entire time, and all effects are achieved solely through adjusting multiple filter cutoffs with LFOs and automation.

  • Sound Design
  • Ableton

Sun Rises West

The chords and melody are all derived from a Native American flute recording. The flute samples are layered and pitched to create a rich texture. I manipulated samples of a deck of cards, a door knob, crunching leaves, and a kalimba to add more percussive interest, rhythm, and sonic contrast.

  • Experimental
  • Ableton
